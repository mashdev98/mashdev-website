Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Mash Dev LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account. Email is used for password recovery and is not visible to other users.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect location data to enable features like automatic pool check-ins.
  • Contacts: With your permission, we read your device contacts on-device to help you discover friends who already use our apps. Phone numbers are compared on your device; we do not upload or store your contact list on our servers.
  • Photos: Photos you choose to use as a profile picture or for family member records, stored securely in our cloud backend.
  • Push Notification Token: A device-specific token used by Apple's push notification service so we can deliver alerts (e.g., friend activity, friend requests). The token does not identify you outside our services.
  • App Activity: Pool check-ins, planned visits, and similar actions you take within the app, shared only with users you have approved as friends.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Enable features like location-based check-ins and friend connections
  • Send you notifications about pool activity (with your consent)
  • Maintain and support our services and respond to your inquiries

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users as part of app functionality (e.g., showing friends you're at the pool)
  • With service providers who assist in operating our services (e.g., cloud hosting)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Update or delete your account information within the app
  • Disable location services through your device settings
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
