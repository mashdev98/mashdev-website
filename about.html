About Mash Dev LLC

A mobile app development company based in Bethesda, Maryland

Our Story

Mash Dev LLC was founded in 2026 with a simple mission: to create mobile applications that bring people together and strengthen real-world communities.

We believe technology should enhance human connection, not replace it. That's why we focus on building apps that help neighbors, families, and friends coordinate and stay connected in their everyday lives.

Our Approach

We take a user-first approach to app development. Every feature we build starts with a real problem that real people face. We listen to our users, iterate quickly, and focus on creating intuitive experiences that just work.

Our apps are built with privacy and security in mind. We collect only the data necessary to provide our services, and we never sell user information to third parties.

Technology

We specialize in native iOS development using Swift and SwiftUI, ensuring our apps feel fast, responsive, and at home on Apple devices. Our backend infrastructure is built on modern cloud services that provide reliability and scalability.

  • Native iOS development with Swift and SwiftUI
  • Cloud-based backend services
  • Real-time data synchronization
  • Location-aware features with privacy controls
  • Push notifications for timely updates

Looking Ahead

We're just getting started. Our first app, PoolCheck, is currently in development and will be launching soon on the App Store. We have more ideas in the pipeline and are excited to continue building tools that help communities thrive.
